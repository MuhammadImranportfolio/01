About me

I am an MPhil Biotechnology researcher exploring the potential of microalgae for sustainable agriculture, environmental biotechnology and climate solutions. My research focuses on algae-based biostimulants, indigenous microalgal strains, carbon capture and sustainable resource recovery.

I am interested in research collaborations and opportunities in microalgae biotechnology, sustainable agriculture, climate innovation and the circular bioeconomy.
