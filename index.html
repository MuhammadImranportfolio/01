Research Direction

Microalgae as platforms for sustainable biotechnology

Microalgae represent a diverse biological resource with significant potential for sustainable agricultural and environmental applications.

Research in microalgal biotechnology increasingly connects indigenous strain diversity, cultivation, biochemical composition, biomass processing, and biological activity.

This research direction focuses on understanding how microalgal biomass and its bioactive components can contribute to sustainable agricultural systems, while also exploring opportunities in carbon capture and resource recovery.

About

Research-driven biotechnology

I am an M.Phil. Biotechnology researcher with an academic focus on microalgal biotechnology and sustainable biological solutions.

My research interests include indigenous microalgal strains, algae-based biostimulants, biochemical profiling, biomass extraction, plant-growth evaluation, carbon capture, and sustainable resource recovery.

A major area of interest is understanding the biological potential of indigenous microalgae and how their biochemical characteristics can be connected to sustainable agricultural applications.

My academic environment is connected with the BioEcoTech Research Cluster (BERC) at Government College University Faisalabad, where microalgal biotechnology and sustainable innovation are important research themes.
