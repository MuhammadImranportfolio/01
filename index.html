ABOUT ME

Biotechnology with a sustainability perspective.

I am an M.Phil. Researcher in Biotechnology with an academic focus on microalgal biotechnology and sustainable biological systems.

My interests include indigenous microalgal strains, algae-based biostimulants, biochemical profiling, biomass extraction, plant-growth evaluation, carbon capture and sustainable resource recovery.

I am particularly interested in research that connects biological resources with practical solutions for sustainable agriculture and environmental sustainability.
