About

Research-driven biotechnology

I am an M.Phil. Biotechnology researcher with an academic interest in microalgal biotechnology and sustainable biological solutions.

My academic interests focus on indigenous microalgal strains, algae-based biostimulants, biochemical profiling, biomass extraction, plant-growth evaluation, carbon capture, and sustainable resource recovery.

My broader goal is to understand how the biological diversity and biochemical potential of microalgae can be translated into useful applications for sustainable agriculture and environmental biotechnology.

I am particularly interested in research that connects fundamental biological understanding with scalable and environmentally relevant applications.
