ABOUT ME

Biotechnology research driven by sustainability.

I am a Biotechnology researcher with an academic background in Industrial and Environmental Biotechnology, currently pursuing an M.Phil. at Government College University, Faisalabad.

My research interests center on indigenous microalgae, microalgal cultivation, biomass processing, extraction, biochemical profiling and the development of algae-based biostimulants for sustainable agriculture.

I am also interested in environmental biotechnology, carbon capture, sustainable resource recovery and circular bioeconomy approaches that connect biological systems with real-world sustainability challenges.
