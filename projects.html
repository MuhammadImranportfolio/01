Research projects

Assessing the biostimulant activity of indigenous microalgal extract for sustainable agriculture

2025 to 2026, Bioenergy Research Centre

Investigating the biostimulant potential of indigenous microalgal extract for sustainable agriculture. The work covers extract preparation and its effects on plant growth and nutrient efficiency. Done with Bisma.

  • Microalgae Cultivation
  • Microalgae-Based Biostimulants

Biological synthesis of ZnO nanoparticles from olive leaf extract and their antimicrobial effect against Pseudomonas aeruginosa

Mar 2024 to Jun 2024, Government College University, Faisalabad

Olive leaves were used instead of more toxic chemicals to make the nanoparticles, which is safer for people and the environment. Pseudomonas aeruginosa resists multiple antibiotics, but in this project its growth was inhibited by the nanoparticles.

  • Green synthesis
  • Nanoparticles
  • Antimicrobial activity
